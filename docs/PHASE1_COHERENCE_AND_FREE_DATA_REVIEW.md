# Phase 1 — critical coherence and free-dataset review

**2026-11-03. Status: RECOMMENDED SCIENTIFIC/PRODUCT REVISION, NOT OWNER-APPROVED.** No assessment of existing code, UI or current data was performed. The prior 35 detailed methods and all owner-approved ten intentions remain unchanged pending final Phase 1 acceptance.

## Verdict

Keep the seven interdisciplinary research domains. **Revise the default narrative:** the six current overview views are individually plausible but do not explain global activity → monetary transmission → vulnerabilities → valuations/distribution → diversification. The catalogue underweights property, inflation shocks, funding and non-bank intermediation, global inequality and global value-chain transmission, while some existing questions duplicate methods. Free-source-only needs to be a **binding source-selection constraint** rather than a future optimization.

**Hard rule: no paid datasets, paid APIs, commercial index terminals or required subscriptions.** Free to read is NOT the same as licensed to automatically download, store, reuse or redistribute. Use official no-fee source files and non-commercial personal research rights, recording original-source terms and attribution. A free narrower approximation must use a **different correct name**; it must never fill the slot of an unavailable original with a false label.

## Six connected overview research experiences

Each experience displays one legible primary comparison, optional 1–3 aligned supporting panels, two dated observed-evidence sentences, contradictory data, limitations and "Explore / Adjust / Method / Sources". A research story comprises multiple statistically compatible graphs; it is not a global score or a wall of cards. All geographical choices are **per graph**, and saved graphs do not alter unrelated charts.

| Order | Research question | Primary comparison | Support and explicit boundary |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1 — Global activity** | Is growth slowing, and are inflation/commodity shocks emerging? | Country-matched real growth US, Europe, Japan, China, other available regions; clearly mark observed versus IMF projected years | OECD CLI (growth-cycle, not GDP forecast), energy and inflation, NBER US-only historical chronology |
| **2 — Monetary transmission** | How do central banks and discount conditions differ? | Fed/ECB/BoJ policy instruments plus separate same-maturity nominal/real yield panels | FX changes and stress; no universal policy-stance number or FX causal inference |
| **3 — Fragility** | Are debt, property collateral and funding exposures worsening? | BIS national credit-gap and within-country DSR deviations in synchronized panels | Residential/commercial property cycles, bank/non-bank leverage, funding/liquidity; different predictive horizons |
| **4 — Valuation/concentration** | Are equity markets expensive or concentrated? | US CAPE and real yields, with separately labelled comparable regional index evidence where free | Public index/ETF concentration histories only for their true universes; no imputed MSCI holdings or fake observed equity risk premium |
| **5 — Production/distribution** | Who receives the proceeds of productivity and capital accumulation? | Same-sector productivity vs real average pay; labour vs operating-surplus components | OECD IOT/STAN profitability assumptions, WID/WIID global inequality and alternative Marxian methods |
| **6 — Long-horizon portfolio** | How do global conditions propagate to diversified investments? | Matched stocks/bonds/gold returns, rolling correlations and drawdowns | Optional US/UCITS fund exposure with dated genuine holdings; currency, rebalancing, costs, inflation, conditional scenarios—not trading instructions |

No analysis is entitled to a homepage place solely because the data exist. The six are curated evidence **stories**, while the full circa-35-analysis library remains a question-indexed research workbench.

## Four narrowly defined source searches where owner assistance could help

No reason to ask the user to rediscover BIS, OECD, FRED, World Bank, NBER, IMF or SEC pages—the original datasets are already located.

1. A **free, legally usable, original historical global index constituent/issuer-weights series**, preferably 10+ years monthly or quarterly, with universe, ISIN/issuer and real weights. *Current MSCI factsheets are not enough*.
2. **Dated historical UCITS holdings** for actual European funds (e.g. VWCE/VWRL/EUNL, or comparable official iShares funds), covering at least 2015/2020/2024/2026 with full constituents/weights, disclosure/availability dates, permitted file use. *Current holdings alone are not enough*.
3. **Yearly global listed-parent consolidated net income** with actual parent-issuer deduplication, financial currency, fiscal-year labels and reproducible global coverage. *National-account gross corporate surplus cannot substitute*.
4. A **free original monthly global equity net-total-return and investable global bond total-return benchmark**, historically comparable and legally usable in a noncommercial local dashboard. Academic US annual returns or ETFs may serve separate labelled research but not exact indexes.

If a candidate is found, provide **original publisher landing URL, a real data download/file, published history date span and terms**. A paywalled academic paper, index factsheet, reseller preview or generic data-directory entry alone does not satisfy the contract.

## Scientific interface critique and revised acceptance requirements

**The existing proposed visualization contract has many settings but insufficient editorial structure.** The new user flow must be:

**Question → one clear primary comparison → synchronized support → dated evidence and conflicting signals → alternative scientific explanation → observed risk relevance → optional holdings/portfolio-specific scenario**.

- One stable **Overview** of six substantive research stories. Use prominent wide, readable line comparisons; evidence and units visible; secondary data panels expandable.
- **Research workbench** searches *questions* first, then concepts/geographies/papers. Each approved analysis can be duplicated, saved and configured per-figure; there is no global country mode.
- **Per-chart editor** offers source/indicator selection, independent region(s), period/vintage, true measured units, transformations, figure type, documented event annotations. Disallow invalid stacking/mixed axes with a specific explanation. **Line** for dense time series, bars/dots for categories, scatter for matched associations, heatmaps for comparable matrices and aligned small multiples for incompatible units.
- **Method tab** follows the actual modified figure: formula and original numerator/denominator, sector/region, original sources and licenses, observation/first-publication/revision dates, missing points, competing theories and independent forecast evidence.
- **Historical periods:** source-published NBER US March–April 2020 recession displayed with its actual short duration; a different, separately justified pandemic shock and series-dependent recovery-to-previous-level overlay can continue much longer.
- **Uncertainty:** no unsupported “global risk score”, generic up/down color badges, current-data estimates disguised as vintage observations, fabricated research interpretations or paid-series substitutes.
- **Readability:** question, evidence and plot precede methods/admin. Legible axes and line encodings in light/dark and desktop/mobile. Admin sync and developer logs stay secondary.

**Normative acceptance walkthrough:** Configure BIS credit-gap/DSR figure to Germany and Japan, add real estate context, inspect original population and source dates, save it. Fed/ECB/BoJ graph on the same overview retains independent region/series/period settings. All plots remain scientifically valid and explain missingness.
